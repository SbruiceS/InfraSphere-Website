Platform Overview

InfraSphere is designed as an independent, self-hosted infrastructure control plane. It provides a unified operational model for desired-state management, visibility, and governance across heterogeneous environments.

Capabilities

Multi-Environment Management

Centralized operational model across public cloud, virtualization, containers, and on-premises.

Inputs: environment definitions. Outputs: unified inventory and change coordination.

Desired-State Management

Express intended configuration once and maintain it as the source of operational intent.

Inputs: desired specifications. Outputs: comparison baseline for observed state.

Continuous Reconciliation

Identify differences between desired and observed state and support controlled alignment.

Inputs: desired and observed snapshots. Outputs: drift reports and change proposals.

Policy and Governance

Evaluate changes against organizational policy before they are applied.

Inputs: proposed changes. Outputs: allow / deny / require-approval decisions.

Infrastructure Visibility

Understand resource relationships, change impact, and operational health.

Inputs: inventory and observations. Outputs: dependency and health views.

Hybrid Operations

Apply one operational model across cloud and on-premises infrastructure.

Inputs: mixed environment targets. Outputs: consistent control and reporting.
