Platform Overview

InfraSphere is an independent, self-hosted infrastructure control plane designed to provide a unified operational model across public clouds, virtualization platforms, container environments, and on-premises infrastructure.

Capabilities

Unified Infrastructure Control

Centralized operational visibility and management across heterogeneous infrastructure environments.

Desired-State Management

Define intended configurations and compare them against observed infrastructure states.

Continuous Reconciliation

Identify configuration drift and support controlled alignment between desired and observed states.

Policy and Governance

Establish consistent policy evaluation, governance boundaries, and operational controls.

Infrastructure Intelligence

Visibility into dependencies, configuration changes, operational health, and resource relationships.

Hybrid Infrastructure Operations

Unified management approach across cloud, virtualization, container platforms, and on-premises environments.

Auditability

Change history and operational accountability as design objectives.

Desired-State Reconciliation

Intended operational model. Distinguishes the design goal from currently implemented functionality.

  1. Define Desired State
  2. Discover Observed State
  3. Compare Configuration
  4. Detect Drift
  5. Evaluate Policies
  6. Authorize Changes
  7. Reconcile and Verify

This flow describes the intended model. Full closed-loop reconciliation across all environments is a design objective, not a claim of current complete implementation.

Competitive Differentiation

InfraSphere is positioned as an independent, self-hosted control plane focused on ownership, multi-environment consistency, and governance. It is not an application runtime platform or a single-IaC orchestrator.
