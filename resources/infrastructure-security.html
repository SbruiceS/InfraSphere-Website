Infrastructure Security

A self-hosted control plane is designed so organizations retain ownership of their operational and security boundaries. No certifications or compliance attestations are claimed.

Design Objectives

  • Self-hosted ownership of the control plane
  • Identity and access boundaries
  • Least-privilege principles
  • Secure handling of provider credentials
  • Auditability of changes
