Multi-Cloud Infrastructure Management

Organizations run workloads across multiple public clouds. Without a unified model, each environment is managed through separate consoles, leading to inconsistent policy and limited visibility.

Challenges

Different APIs, identity systems, and resource models make it difficult to apply the same governance and operational processes everywhere. Fragmented tooling increases the risk of configuration drift and uncontrolled change.

Unified Approach

A multi-cloud control plane aims to present one inventory, one desired-state model, and one policy evaluation path across providers while still respecting each environment’s native capabilities.
