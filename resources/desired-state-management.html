Desired-State Infrastructure Management

Desired state is the authoritative declaration of what infrastructure should look like. It serves as the comparison baseline for the actual observed configuration.

Declarative Model

Instead of issuing a sequence of imperative commands, operators declare the intended end state. The control plane then identifies differences between that declaration and reality.

Comparison

Comparison reveals missing resources, unexpected resources, and configuration mismatches. These differences become the input for reconciliation and policy evaluation.
