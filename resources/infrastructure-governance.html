Infrastructure Policy and Governance

Governance ensures infrastructure changes stay within approved boundaries through policy evaluation, authorization, and auditability.

Policy Evaluation

Proposed changes are checked against organizational rules before they are applied. Outcomes can include allow, deny, or require-approval.

Access Boundaries

Least-privilege and role-based access are design goals so only authorized operators can initiate or approve changes.
