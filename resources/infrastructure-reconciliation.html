Infrastructure Reconciliation

Reconciliation is the process of bringing observed infrastructure closer to the declared desired state under policy control.

Lifecycle

  1. Define desired state
  2. Collect observed state
  3. Compare configurations
  4. Identify drift
  5. Evaluate policy
  6. Authorize changes
  7. Align and verify

Full closed-loop reconciliation across all environments is a design objective, not a claim of complete current implementation.
